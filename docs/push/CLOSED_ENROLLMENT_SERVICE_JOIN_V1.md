# Closed enrollment service join and retirement guards

This source change composes the previously qualified single-use enrollment factory into the existing **internal** surface service. Public dispatch remains compiled off; issuer and Activity admission profiles remain empty. No real enrollment, key, provider, native, remote migration or deployment is authorized.

The factory captures its original environment, database, operator-secret value, clock callback and currentness callback. A guarded async thunk checks the original identity and request cancellation before starting work and after completion. Every authorization, redemption and withdrawal batch constructs its statements, then checks again immediately before submission without awaiting. Readback and positive credential/receipt construction receive the same guards. The composed service captures the original AbortSignal before awaiting the handler and rechecks cancellation after that await, directly before returning the response. Authentication uses the immutable original secret snapshot. Observed service-identity loss, callback replacement, close or an unqualified clock permanently retires that instance; restoring old values cannot re-enable it. Currentness must represent the original monotonic generation. Sampling cannot detect an unobserved false-then-true transition and is not claimed to do so.

A submitted D1 batch may commit before retirement or cancellation is observed. This change suppresses subsequent positive results and credentials; it does not pretend to cancel or roll back an already submitted transaction. Existing atomic once/claim/SQL-deadline fences remain unchanged. Service closure is not delegation withdrawal, and no automatic retry, new UUID, credential replay, grant renewal or authority reconstruction is introduced.
